Key Business Findings

Statistical patterns discovered from 10,000 customer records that reveal the true drivers of churn.

📍
Germany Churn Rate
32.4%
German customers churn at nearly double the rate of French (16.2%) and Spanish (16.7%) customers — a critical regional risk factor.
👥
Gender Gap
25.1% vs 16.5%
Female customers churn at 25.1% vs 16.5% for males — an 8.6 percentage point gap demanding gender-specific retention programs.
⚡
Inactive Member Risk
2× Higher
Inactive members churn at twice the rate of active ones. Engagement-based early-warning systems can intercept these customers.
🏦
Balance Paradox
High Balance = Risk
Counterintuitively, customers with higher balances churn more — suggesting they have more options and feel underserved relative to their wealth.
🎂
Age is #1 Predictor
Age 45-55
Middle-aged customers (45-55) represent the highest churn cohort. They likely have more complex financial needs that aren't being met.
📦
Product Trap
3-4 Products
Customers with 3 or 4 products have an extremely high churn rate — over-selling creates friction and dissatisfaction rather than loyalty.
